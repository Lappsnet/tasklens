TaskLens: your repair, quoted in seconds.

Look at the problem with your phone and describe it out loud. TaskLens AI
builds a quote with a price range and materials list, shows verified nearby
pros with price, distance and rating, and books your slot.

How it works:
1. Look — photograph the damage with your camera.
2. Speak — describe the problem by voice or text.
3. Book — pick a pro, sign the contract, authorize the escrow hold.
4. Verify — payment is released only when before/after photos pass
   verification. If anything fails, request a fix or open a dispute.

For pros: get jobs with clear scope and photos before you arrive, get paid
with guaranteed escrow, and build your reputation with verified ratings.

Categories: plumbing, electrical, appliances, drywall & paint, construction,
assembly, cleaning and installations.

PayPal payments (card or balance), in USD. Your photos stay private and are
shared only with your assigned pro.
