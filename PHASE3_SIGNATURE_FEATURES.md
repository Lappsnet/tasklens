# TaskLens — Signature features (the "astonishing" layer)

Run AFTER `PHASE2_PROMPT.md` blocks P0-1 (real persistence + keys) and P0-2 (PayPal escrow).
Everything here builds on real data and real payments; on mock data it would fall apart
in front of judges. Then build these in waves, interleaved with P0-3/P0-4 frontend work.

Product thesis: TaskLens is not a directory of handymen. It is the **operating system for
getting physical work done**: from the first glance at a problem, to a signed contract,
a scheduled crew, a verified result, and a permanent record of the home.

Every feature below must (1) use at least one Meta model in a way visible on screen,
(2) show up in the demo video, (3) follow AGENTS.md section 7.

## WAVE 1 (Oct 30 – Oct 31): the contract-to-calendar spine

### S1. Contract Studio — AI contracts, e-signature, change orders
The moment that turns a quote into a deal.
- After a pro is chosen, Muse Spark drafts a **bilingual service agreement (ES/EN)** from
  the `ai_spec` + booking: parties, address, scope (each step), exclusions, materials and
  who supplies them, price and payment terms (escrow, release on verification), schedule,
  warranty period (default 30 days labor, editable by pro), cancellation, change-order
  clause, dispute process.
- Contract is generated from a **fixed clause library** (`packages/ai/contracts/clauses/*.md`)
  that Muse Spark fills and selects; it never invents clauses outside the library.
  Output validated by Zod (`ContractDraft`).
- Both parties review side by side with a **plain-language summary** ("What you're agreeing
  to, in 5 lines") generated by Muse Spark.
- **E-signature**: typed name + drawn signature (`signature_pad`), email one-time code
  confirmation, timestamp, IP, user agent. Final PDF (`@react-pdf/renderer`) is hashed
  (SHA-256); hash + signatures written to `job_events`. PDF in the private `media` bucket.
- **Escrow is only authorized after both signatures.**
- **Change orders**: mid-job, the pro looks at an unexpected issue through the glasses
  ("the pipe behind the wall is also corroded") -> Muse Spark drafts a change order
  (added scope, price delta, time delta) -> customer approves with one tap + signature ->
  PayPal additional authorization for the delta. The job log shows the amended contract.
- Footer on every contract: "Plantilla generada con IA; no constituye asesoría legal."
  Owner must have a lawyer review the clause library before live (non-sandbox) use.
- Tables: `contracts`, `contract_signatures`, `change_orders`.
- Demo moment: contract appears in 3 seconds, both sign on two phones, escrow locks.

### S2. Smart Calendar — AI scheduling and the pro's day plan
- Pro calendar (`/pro/calendar`): week view, drag to block time, recurring availability,
  buffer times, service zones.
- **AI slot suggestions**: for a customer, Muse Spark proposes the 3 best slots using
  urgency, the pro's existing jobs, and travel time (Mapbox Directions / Matrix API).
- **AI day planner**: each morning the pro gets an optimised route of the day's jobs with
  ETAs, materials checklist for all jobs combined, and a spoken briefing on the glasses
  ("3 jobs today, first in Santa Tecla at 8:30, bring a 1.5-inch P-trap").
- **Calendar sync without OAuth**: a private ICS feed per user (`/api/calendar/<token>.ics`,
  long random token, revocable) that subscribes in Google, Apple and Outlook calendars;
  `.ics` attachment on every booking email. Customer gets the same for their jobs.
- Reschedule flow with mutual approval; auto-reauthorize PayPal holds when a job moves
  more than 3 days out.
- Tables: `calendar_blocks`, `calendar_feeds` (token hash only).
- Demo moment: pro says "plan my day" to the glasses, hears the route, the map animates.

### S3. Home Passport — every repair becomes a permanent record
- Every verified job writes a **Home Passport** entry: before/after photos, signed contract,
  materials used, pro, warranty expiry, AI summary.
- **Maintenance radar**: Muse Spark reads the passport and proposes reminders
  ("Revisar el calentador en 12 meses") with one-tap booking when due.
- **Warranty claims**: within the warranty window, the customer scans the same spot ->
  Muse Spark compares with the "after" photo -> opens a warranty job with the original pro
  at no charge.
- **Share/export**: a PDF passport for selling or renting the home; share link with expiry.
- Tables: `homes`, `home_members`, `passport_entries`, `warranties`, `reminders`.
- Demo moment: the finished leak repair lands in the passport with its warranty countdown.

## WAVE 2 (Nov 1): bigger jobs, money from anywhere, fair disputes

### S4. Project Planner — from a walkthrough video to a crew
For construction and remodels, not just fixes.
- Customer records a 30–60 s **walkthrough video** (phone or glasses) and says what they
  want ("quiero remodelar este baño"). Muse Spark (video input) returns a `ProjectPlan`:
  phases in trade order (demolition -> plumbing -> electrical -> drywall -> tile -> paint),
  per-phase scope, duration, bill of materials with quantities, budget range, risks.
  Fallback: sample 8 frames if the video is too large.
- **Design preview**: Muse Image renders 3 finish options (tile, paint, fixtures) on the
  customer's own photo; customer picks one and it is attached to the plan.
- **Crew booking**: one pro per trade, scheduled in sequence on the Smart Calendar with
  dependencies (paint can't start before drywall is verified).
- **Milestone escrow**: one PayPal authorization per phase; each phase is released only
  when its own before/after verification passes. A Gantt-style timeline shows progress.
- Tables: `projects`, `project_phases`, `phase_dependencies`.
- Demo moment: a 40-second bathroom video becomes a 5-phase plan, a render and a crew.

### S5. Pay from abroad — family funding links
Many Salvadoran families have relatives abroad who help pay for home repairs.
- The customer creates a job and taps "Pedir a un familiar que pague" -> a **funding link**
  (single-use, expires in 72 h, shows only job title, price, contract summary and photos;
  never the street address or phone).
- The relative pays the escrow hold with PayPal or card from any country, sees the live
  job timeline and the verified after photos, and receives the receipt and contract.
- Release still follows verification; refunds go back to the payer.
- Tables: `funding_links` (token hash, payer email, status).
- Demo moment: a relative in the US funds a repair in San Salvador and watches it finish.

### S6. Fair-fix mediator — AI-assisted disputes with partial release
- When verification returns `review` or a party disputes, Muse Spark compares the signed
  contract scope, the change orders, and before/after photos step by step, and proposes a
  resolution: redo specific steps, or a **partial capture** (PayPal supports capturing
  less than the authorized amount) with the rest voided.
- Both parties accept or escalate to an admin; every proposal and decision is logged.
- The AI proposes; it never moves money on its own.
- Tables: `disputes`, `dispute_proposals`.

## WAVE 3 (Nov 2, until the 8 PM freeze): the vision features

### S7. TaskLens for Cities — fix the central-repair bottleneck
Directly addresses how repairs work in El Salvador today: requests pile up at one central
office and take days or weeks.
- Residents can mark a scan as a **public issue** (street light, water leak in the street,
  broken sidewalk). Location is snapped to the street; faces and plates are not stored
  (prompt Muse Spark to reject images where people are the subject).
- A **city operator dashboard** (`/city`, new role `city_operator`): AI-triaged queue by
  severity and category, clustered duplicates on a map, suggested certified contractors,
  SLA timers, and before/after proof when closed.
- Positioning: TaskLens does not replace the central office; it gives it a triage and
  dispatch tool and a transparent public status page.
- Tables: `public_reports`, `report_clusters`. RLS: operators see only their municipality.

### S8. Pro Copilot on the glasses — hands-free expert
- During job mode the pro asks questions out loud ("¿qué sellador uso aquí?") and Muse Spark
  answers using the job spec and the current glasses frame, spoken back through the speakers.
- **Hazard watch**: every N seconds a frame is checked for visible hazards (exposed wiring,
  gas fittings, standing water near outlets); a hazard triggers a spoken warning and an
  event in the job log. Advisory only, clearly labelled.
- Auto-generated **step evidence**: when the pro says "listo", the current frame is saved
  as evidence for that step, feeding verification.

## ACROSS ALL FEATURES
- **Trust score** on every pro, computed only from verified data: verified jobs, on-time
  rate, rework rate, warranty claims, rating. Shown with a breakdown, never a black box.
- **Portfolio** auto-built from the pro's verified before/after pairs (real photos only).
- **Bilingual chat** between customer and pro with live ES/EN translation by Muse Spark and
  voice notes transcribed by Muse Voice Transcribe.
- Notifications: email + in-app; WhatsApp share links (no WhatsApp API).

## DATA + SECURITY RULES FOR NEW FEATURES
- New migrations `004_contracts.sql` … one per wave, each with RLS policies in the same file.
- Tokens (ICS feeds, funding links, passport share links) are random 32-byte values; store
  only their SHA-256 hash; expire and revoke.
- Contract PDFs and passport exports: private bucket, 60 s signed URLs, owner or party only.
- All AI outputs (contracts, plans, mediations, triage) are Zod-validated and shown as
  drafts until a human approves; the AI never signs, pays, captures or closes anything.
- Money movement only in server code, only after the signatures/verdicts listed above.
